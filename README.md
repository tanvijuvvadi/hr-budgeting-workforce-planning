# HR Budgeting & Workforce Planning Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

The **HR Budgeting & Workforce Planning Tool** is an industry-oriented HR Analytics project designed to help HR, Finance, HR Business Partners, Workforce Planning teams, Talent Acquisition teams, and business leaders make data-driven workforce decisions.

The project combines **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze workforce structure, headcount, FTE, employee costs, payroll, HR budgets, hiring requirements, attrition replacement needs, workforce gaps, and future workforce scenarios.

The solution is built using **synthetic and anonymized workforce data** and is intended for educational, portfolio, and demonstration purposes.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations need to balance workforce requirements with financial constraints.

HR and Finance teams need answers to questions such as:

- How many employees do we currently have?
- What is our total FTE capacity?
- Which departments have the highest workforce costs?
- How much will payroll cost in the future?
- Are actual workforce costs exceeding the approved budget?
- How many employees need to be hired?
- How much hiring is required because of attrition?
- Which departments have workforce shortages?
- Which roles are critical?
- What will happen to payroll if salaries increase by 5%, 8%, or 10%?
- How would a high-growth scenario affect HR costs?
- Where can internal mobility reduce external hiring?

This project addresses these questions through an integrated workforce planning and HR budgeting model.

The major objectives of the project are:

1. Analyze current workforce structure.
2. Calculate Headcount and FTE.
3. Analyze employee compensation and workforce costs.
4. Build HR budgeting analysis.
5. Compare budgeted vs actual workforce costs.
6. Forecast future workforce demand.
7. Estimate internal workforce supply.
8. Identify workforce gaps.
9. Plan replacement and growth hiring.
10. Analyze recruitment costs.
11. Forecast payroll.
12. Model salary increment scenarios.
13. Analyze attrition-related costs.
14. Plan training expenditure.
15. Compare workforce scenarios.
16. Create an executive HR dashboard.
17. Generate actionable HR and Finance insights.
